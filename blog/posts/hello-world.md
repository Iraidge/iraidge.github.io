Hi, I'm Ivan, a young game developer who wants to pursue gameplay and graphics programming.
I'm starting this blog to track my progress as I learn graphics APIs, gameplay systems, and game design features.

## Where I am now

I really like how low-level programming works. It feels complex until you get pulled into learning it. Right now I'm building small projects with raylib and have started trying out OpenGL.

I'm studying Game Design at Leeds Beckett University and learning C++ in Unreal Engine. So far I've made a couple of personal projects, separate from my uni coursework.

## Where I'm going

- keep building my projects
- understand how the graphics pipeline works
- eventually write my first triangle, or even a small renderer, in Vulkan

## What to expect here

Short posts on things I want to learn, technology I use, games I play, and my thoughts on the game development industry.

There will also be bugs, development logs, and solutions I want to share, so expect some of those. I'll try to post every two weeks.
 
If you want to talk or discuss any of my projects, find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/iraidge).
